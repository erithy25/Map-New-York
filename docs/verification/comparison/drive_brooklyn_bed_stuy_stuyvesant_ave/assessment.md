## What matches

* **The frame is finally the picture the item asks for.** A level view up the roadway centre with a block face, kerb, sidewalk and canopy on both sides. The previous render of this slug was a stoop at a few metres with no street in it; J48 corrected the point to the Stuyvesant Avenue / Decatur Street crossing and snapped the azimuth to the avenue's own bearing, and the clearance probe now reports 150 m of clear axis honestly rather than as an artefact of a 6 deg cone (J47).
* **The curb ramps are cut into the pavement mesh and you can see them.** At the left kerb about 40 m out the reveal tapers to nothing and the sidewalk runs down flush into the gutter, then the kerb resumes past the crossing. 40 surveyed ramps in range are built as pavement rather than as props, which is what `kinds_built_elsewhere` records (J21).
* **The rigged vehicles carry nothing under them.** The four cars standing at the cross street about a block ahead read as plain dark sedan silhouettes on the roadbed; the untextured 2 m sphere that used to sit at every rear axle is gone (J44).
* **The season is right and it is the reference's own season.** 236 of the 364 props are trees and every one is in bare-canopy form, matching the leafless plane trees on 30 November in the photograph, and the sun is placed from that photograph's own EXIF instant.
* **The kerb line and carriageway width are credible for a Bed-Stuy avenue.** A raised kerb with a visible face runs down both sides and the sidewalks are wide enough for the tree pits and a walking group.
* **The street furniture is right.** Cobra-head lamps on curved masts, red hydrants at the kerb, manhole discs in the roadbed, a rank of dock posts on the right sidewalk, a lit blank shopfront fascia under a green awning on the left, and a stoop of two flights with railings over a dropped areaway behind a white fence.

## What does not match

* **The near facades have no windows.** The salmon wall on the right and the cream wall on the left are unbroken planes. On them float pale lintel bars, curved segmental arch hoods at inconsistent angles, and grey through-wall air-conditioners on brackets, with no aperture, no reveal, no frame and no glass anywhere beneath them. 2,458 window pieces are reported placed; on the two walls that fill half this frame, none of them reads.
* **The reference is 1.8 km away and is a different intersection.** The photograph is Nostrand Avenue at Greene, its EXIF GPS 1,808 m from the viewpoint and rejected as mis-tagged. It is also a three-quarter view pitched up at a corner building, not a view along a street, so the two halves share no composition at all and the sheet's own caption says to compare them on street width, storey height and material instead.
* **The roadway is empty.** Not one vehicle stands anywhere in the near hundred metres, and neither kerb carries a parked car. A Bed-Stuy avenue at 10:22 on a Monday is parked bumper to bumper on both sides; the reference photograph has a car at its own kerb. The 77 vehicles the record counts are spread over a 320 m radius in every direction, and about four of them are in this frame.
* **The crossing is a solid white slab.** The crosswalk immediately in front of the camera is one continuous painted rectangle spanning the whole carriageway rather than a bar pattern, and there is no centre line, lane line or parking-lane marking anywhere on 309 roadbed polygons.
* **The people are dressed for July.** Tank tops, bare arms and shorts on both sidewalks, on 30 November, under 24.9 deg of winter sun.
* **The nearest pedestrian shows the character faults close up.** Her hair is a single solid shell down her back (E3), her hands are closed blobs, and the waist of her trousers opens into a hole where garment and body fail to resolve (E8).
* **The roof edge is a jumble.** Bulkhead slabs stack at odd angles along the left parapet and a grey louvred unit hangs past the roof edge into open air, tilted.
* **Surfaces are flat colour.** No brownstone coursing, no brick bond, no asphalt or concrete texture; and the sky is a clean gradient washing to white at the vanishing point where the reference has a deep saturated winter blue.
* **The frame is taken standing in a live carriageway** at 1.6 m in the middle of the road (I14) - the item's own instruction, but not where a photographer stands.
