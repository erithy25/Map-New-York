## Verdict — the chooser traded a GPS-located photograph of this tower for a hard-cropped reflection study, because only the reflection study carries a clock

**The render is a careful attempt at a photograph that is sitting unused in the same folder.** `pick_reference_photo` ranks candidates on the tuple `(lit_bad, band, has_time, lit_tier, conf, err)`, and for a landmark there is no distance term (J60). Candidate 1 in this item's `meta.json` scored **10.45**: it carries camera GPS **55 m** from the subject, a **high**-confidence viewpoint derived from that GPS, and a description that reads *"Looking up at the Times Square Tower (7 Times Square) at 42nd Street and 7th Avenue / Broadway"* — which is exactly the frame the render built. Candidate 3 scored **8.4**, carries no GPS, and its viewpoint is a **medium**-confidence assumption. Candidate 3 won, on one term: its `date_taken` is `2021-09-21 10:42:47` and candidate 1's is the bare year `2018`, so `has_time` ranks above confidence and above azimuth error. The rule is defensible in the abstract — a real EXIF instant fixes the Sun exactly, and this sheet's Sun *is* exact — and on this item it bought an exact Sun at the price of the comparison itself.

**Because what candidate 3 is a photograph of is glass.** The reference frame contains a hard-cropped stretch of curtain wall: bronze-toned mullions and spandrel bands on a module, a re-entrant corner with exposed painted steel diagonal bracing behind the glazing, and the mirrored image of the towers opposite. There is no sky in it, no roofline, no street, no massing — nothing a 149.9 m upward view of a whole building can be laid beside. The render's own caption already declares the frames incomparable on proportion; they are also incomparable on subject matter, and for a reason the build states about itself. `pipeline/nycsim_pipeline/unreal/city_surfaces.py` keeps one surface analytic on purpose and gives the reason in a comment: *"Glass is the whole list: a photograph of a curtain wall is a photograph of what it reflects."* The reference photograph is the proof of that sentence. The render has a procedural Nishita sky at strength 0.0334 and the buildings within 640 m to reflect, and no photograph of Times Square anywhere in the pipeline, so the one thing the left half is made of is the one thing the right half cannot contain.

**Under all of that, the tower itself is built well and measured well.** The height probe cast 43 rays, 25 landed on built fabric, and the answer off `lm_c_times_square.67` — the object standing at the subject's coordinate — is **219.1 m** above a ground of 16.88 m, against the builder's own published 221.0 m from SOM/CTBUH. That is under two metres on a 47-storey tower. It also shows J74 earning its keep in one line: the nearest catalogue origin within 120 m is the composite `c_times_square` at 79.2 m, and the height that entry carries is **365.8 m** — the Bank of America Tower's spire tip, a different building. Taking the catalogue at its word would have over-stated this subject by nearly 150 m. The plan extent measured off the same object is **62.6 by 43.5 m**, which is the block between Broadway, Seventh Avenue, 41st and 42nd.

**And the curtain wall is modelled as a curtain wall, not as a flat face.** `c_times_square.py` builds it with `cc.curtain`: a glass volume recessed 0.06 m, a spandrel band standing 0.2 m proud at every one of 47 floor lines, and vertical mullions 0.20 m wide on a 3.05 m module. That is the rhythm the photograph is a picture of, and the render has it. What it does not have is the colour: the mullions and spandrels are `steel_nirosta`, a bright stainless at RGB 205, 205, 210 with metallic 1.0, where the photograph shows warm bronze-toned metal. Nor the bracing: `cc.curtain` emits verticals and horizontals only, so the diagonal steel that is the most distinctive thing in the left half has nowhere to come from.

**The sightline is honest and slightly unfair to itself.** 13 rays, **9 clear**, **4 on the subject**, **3 into nothing** and exactly **one blocked** — by `prop_lamp_cobra_davit_2`, a cobra-head street lamp **18.4 m** from the lens. So the published visible fraction of **0.308** is not a story about obstruction; the frame is almost entirely clear. It is a story about where the fan points. The fan is 30.52° in vertical half-angle about an aim at the subject's mid-height, and on a 219 m tower seen from 149.9 m that aim sits higher in the frame than the middle of the tower does, so the fan's top ray clears the roof and its bottom ray never reaches the base. The record also shows the continuity rule working: one ray met the tower's own fabric at **153.1 m**, inside the 160.5 m at which a hit would otherwise have counted as an obstruction, and `subject_rays_on_own_fabric_nearer_than_recorded` counts it as the subject rather than against it.

## What matches

* **The height, to under two metres.** 219.1 m measured off the object at the coordinate against the builder's published 221.0 m, from 43 rays of which 25 found fabric.
* **The plan extent.** 62.6 by 43.5 m, `is_tile_mesh: false` — a real object's bounding box, not a joined tile mesh (J94).
* **The curtain-wall rhythm.** 47 spandrel bands standing 0.2 m proud and mullions on a 3.05 m module, which is the read the reference photograph is entirely composed of.
* **The tone, to a sixteenth of a stop.** The render's median sits 0.235 stops from the grey convention and the photograph's 0.292, a difference of **−0.057 stops**; p50 0.4976 against 0.5068 (**0.982×**), mean 0.4885 against 0.5072 (**0.963×**). Ninth closest of the 171 sheets whose halves are both measured against the render that made them.
* **The colour is close, for once.** Chroma 0.0683 against 0.0787, a ratio of **0.868** — because the photograph's subject is a blue-grey glass wall and `glass_blue` at RGB 80, 112, 136 with roughness 0.06 is a fair account of low-e curtain-wall glass.
* **The Sun is the real Sun of the real instant**, placed from EXIF rather than assumed, and the frame is metered rather than guessed: 1.149 stops with nothing clamped.
* **Times Square is paved as Times Square**: 25,847 polygons with 9,117 white markings, 6,398 sidewalk, 4,456 roadbed, 3,299 curb, 1,815 plaza and 488 crosswalk, and **0 dropped**.
* **The block is furnished.** 130 Citi Bike dock units, 106 cooling towers, 85 street lamps, 77 manholes, 58 hydrants, 24 subway vent grates, 15 subway entrances, 11 bike racks, 9 benches, 8 bus-stop signs, 6 steam vents, 5 LinkNYC kiosks and 5 newsstands — 1,693 of 1,800 in range, nothing lost to budget.
* **The park ground under this frame is correct where it can be seen.** Within 150 m, 104 samples, `under_frac` **0.0** and a minimum clearance of 0.045 m: not one park surface sinks under the terrain in the near field.
* **The camera did not have to be moved.** `moved: false`, offset 0.0 m, 142.5 m of clear view against a 75.0 m requirement.
* **The clearance walk's continuity rule holds.** A ray landing on the tower's own fabric at 153.1 m is counted as the subject, not as something in the way.

## What does not match

* **The reference is a facade crop, not a view of the building.** No sky, no roofline, no street, no massing. Nothing about proportion, height, setback or silhouette can be read across the two halves, and the render's own caption says so for a different reason (the 18 mm floor).
* **The photograph is a picture of reflections and the render has no photograph to reflect.** Glass is deliberately analytic, the sky is a procedural Nishita dome at strength 0.0334, and no image of Times Square exists anywhere in the pipeline.
* **The mullions are the wrong metal.** `steel_nirosta` at RGB 205, 205, 210 at metallic 1.0, described in the palette as Nirosta stainless, against the photograph's bronze-toned anodised aluminium.
* **The diagonal bracing is absent.** `cc.curtain` emits a glass volume, horizontal spandrel bands and vertical mullions, and nothing diagonal.
* **Two thirds of the kit in range never reached the frame**: 4,922 pieces drawn of **13,354** in range, with **8,432 suppressed under the landmark shells** — the sixth largest such suppression in the pass. The kit was not capped; the pieces were withheld because a landmark shell already stands there.
* **The Times Square video screens are blank.** `c_times_square.py` puts two screen slots on this tower's face between 16.0 and 58.0 m, and the material is `TSQ_SCREEN_<n>`, emissive at strength 6.0 on a base of RGB 230, 230, 235, for the engine to bind a video to. Unbound, they render as white light. 86 billboard kit pieces in the frame are blank for the same declared reason (DEVIATIONS B15a).
* **No structures on any tile.** 0 imported, **4 without a file**, 0 triangles, on the block above the busiest subway interchange in the system. This is not unique: 19 of the 171 records in the pass report the same.
* **Not one tree is drawn from modelled branches** within 120 m; all 1,148 in range are six-triangle impostor cards, 660 of them a substituted species, 1,146 scaled at a mean of 0.914 with 2 outside the band.
* **Sixteen props across four kinds had no asset**: 8 misc structure, 4 artwork, 3 drinking fountain, 1 passenger-information sign. A further 20 were dropped on a suppressed building.
* **The visible fraction of 0.308 under-reads the frame.** One ray of 13 is blocked and it is blocked by a street lamp; 3 of the remaining misses are sky, because the vertical fan is aimed above the tower's middle. The clear fraction of **0.692** is the honest number for this frame.
* **Fifteen of the 53 vehicles are green boro taxis**, in Times Square, where a Street Hail Livery may not legally take a street hail. The pipeline's own fleet table knows this and the runtime does not (J105, new).
* **289 people at 10:42 on a Tuesday in Times Square.** The density table asked for 1,195 vehicles and 2,279 people over the simulated ring; 1,336 and 2,830 were simulated and **3,824 dropped** — 1,175 pedestrians and 569 vehicles at the 1,125,000-triangle agent budget, 1,078 and 642 outside the radius, 177 in the carriageway without crossing, **110 not on a walkable surface** (J101), 39 riderless vehicle bodies, 33 off the carriageway and 1 inside a building.
* **The park ground sinks beyond 400 m.** 1,054 samples, `under_frac` 0.2638, more than 0.05 m under on 0.2258 of them, and a worst case of −1.495 m where the terrain grid coarsens to 40.0 m.
* **The frustum test is useless here again.** It reports "Times Square at 221.3 m, off axis 85.7°" — the composite's centroid, not the member being looked at (J99).
* **No cloud, no haze.** Nothing in this build reads a historical sky; the render's standard deviation is 0.2374 against the photograph's 0.1902 (**1.248×**), which is what an unclouded procedural sky and a hard shadow edge do to a frame.
